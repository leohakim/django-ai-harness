# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in django-ai-harness a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for Django DX

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, and publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers may warn, remove contributions, or ban participants who violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
